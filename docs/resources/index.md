# Resources

Ready-to-use resources for building and managing your FDE program. These templates and tools cover immediate needs for hiring, onboarding, and establishing effective working relationships between FDE and Product teams.

---

## People Resources

Templates for hiring and onboarding FDEs:

### [Job Description Template](people/job-description-fde.md)

Create job postings for FDE roles at different levels (Associate, Senior, Staff, Principal).

**When to use:**

- Opening new FDE positions
- Defining role expectations and requirements
- Communicating role scope internally

---

### [Interview Scorecard Template](people/interview-scorecard.md)

Structured evaluation framework for assessing FDE candidates during interviews.

**When to use:**

- During the interview process
- Standardizing candidate evaluation
- Ensuring consistent assessment across interviewers

---

### [Onboarding Checklist Template](people/onboarding-checklist.md)

Comprehensive checklist for onboarding new FDEs in their first 30-60-90 days.

**When to use:**

- When a new FDE joins
- Planning onboarding programs
- Tracking new hire progress

---

## Product Resources

Templates for managing FDE-Product team interfaces:

### [Interface Agreement Template](product/interface-agreement-template.md)

Framework for establishing clear expectations and working agreements between FDE and Product teams.

**When to use:**

- Starting a new FDE-Product collaboration
- Resolving recurring friction points
- Clarifying ownership boundaries and escalation paths

---

## Operations Resources

Templates for running your FDE function:

### [Operating Model Canvas](operations/operating-model-canvas.md)

One-page template for defining how your FDE function operates — team structure, deployment model, capacity planning, feedback loops, and success metrics.

**When to use:**

- Standing up a new FDE function
- Reviewing an existing operating model that's not working
- Aligning leadership on how the FDE org should run

---

## Using These Resources

All resources are designed to be:

- **Practical** - Based on real-world FDE program patterns
- **Customizable** - Adapt to your organization's context
- **Actionable** - Ready to use with minimal modification

Each resource includes:

- Purpose and when to use it
- Structure and key sections
- Guidance on customization
