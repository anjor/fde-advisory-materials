## Practice Area Summaries

### 1. Organizational Design & Structure

**The Challenge:** Organizations transitioning from traditional consulting/services models struggle to define how FDEs fit within their existing structure and how they interface with other teams.

**Core Topics:** Team topology definition • FDE role definition • Sizing & ratios • Specialized roles within FDE

**→ [Read the full guide](people/organizational-design.md)** | Templates: [Org Structure](templates/people/org-structure-templates.md), [RACI Matrix](templates/shared/raci-matrix-template.md)

---

### 2. Operating Model & Metrics

**The Challenge:** Organizations lack frameworks for measuring FDE effectiveness and establishing governance processes that balance speed with standardization.

**Core Topics:** Organizational metrics • Individual performance management • Revenue thresholds & deal qualification • Rotation & burnout prevention • Backlog governance & prioritization • Client satisfaction measurement

**→ [Read the full guide](operations/operating-model-metrics.md)** | Templates: [Metrics Dashboard](templates/operations/metrics-dashboard-spec.md), [Governance Process](templates/operations/governance-process.md)

---

### 3. Hiring & Talent Strategy

**The Challenge:** Organizations struggle to identify, assess, and develop FDE talent, often defaulting to traditional engineering hiring processes that miss critical FDE competencies.

**Core Topics:** Ideal candidate profile • Multi-channel hiring strategy • Interview process design • Fresh graduate & early career hiring • Interview calibration & training • Compensation & offer strategy

**→ [Read the full guide](people/hiring-talent-strategy.md)** | Templates: [Job Descriptions](templates/people/job-description-fde.md), [Interview Scorecard](templates/people/interview-scorecard.md)

---

### 4. Training, Onboarding & Career Development

**The Challenge:** Even great hires fail without proper onboarding, and retention suffers when career paths are unclear. Organizations need structured programs to develop and retain FDE talent.

**Core Topics:** First 90 days onboarding • Technical ramp-up • Client skills development • Career ladder & progression • Career path options • Retention strategies

**→ [Read the full guide](people/training-career-development.md)** | Templates: [90-Day Onboarding](templates/people/onboarding-checklist.md), [Career Ladder](templates/people/career-ladder-template.md)

---

### 5. Commercial Model & Go-to-Market

**The Challenge:** Organizations accustomed to T&M or staff augmentation models need guidance transitioning to outcome-based commercial structures that align with FDE value delivery.

**Core Topics:** Pricing structure design • Sales motion evolution • FDE role in pre-sales • Outcome-based engagement structure • Promise management & qualification • Contract structures

**→ [Read the full guide](commercial/commercial-model-gtm.md)** | Templates: [Pricing Models](templates/commercial/pricing-model-templates.md), [MSA Template](templates/commercial/msa-template.md)

---

### 6. Product-FDE Interface

**The Challenge:** The boundary between product development and FDE customization is unclear, leading to friction, duplicated effort, and slow feedback loops.

**Core Topics:** FDE contribution to product • Product team as early FDEs • Platform optimization philosophy • Feedback loop mechanisms • Roadmap influence • Ownership & accountability boundaries

**→ [Read the full guide](product/product-fde-interface.md)** | Templates: [Interface Agreement](templates/product/interface-agreement-template.md)

---

### 7. Technical Enablement & Standards

**The Challenge:** FDEs cannot move quickly when documentation is missing, code is disorganized, and development environments are difficult to set up.

**Core Topics:** Documentation standards • Development environment • AI-assisted development • Code quality baseline • Demo infrastructure • Learning by doing • Security & compliance

**→ [Read the full guide](product/technical-enablement.md)** | Templates: [Documentation Standards](templates/product/documentation-templates.md)

---

### 8. Customer Deployment & Success

**The Challenge:** Organizations lack structured approaches for pilot implementations, production deployments, and ongoing customer success post-launch.

**Core Topics:** Pilot structure & execution • Data integration patterns • Production deployment • Client environment considerations • Steady state operations • Customer health monitoring

**→ [Read the full guide](commercial/customer-deployment-success.md)** | Templates: [Pilot Playbook](templates/commercial/pilot-playbook.md)

---

### 9. Change Management & Organizational Adoption

**The Challenge:** Existing teams may resist FDE models—product teams see FDEs as threats, engineering teams worry about code quality, sales teams don't understand how to position. Successful FDE transformation requires deliberate change management.

**Core Topics:** Stakeholder analysis • Product team buy-in • Sales team enablement • Engineering culture shift • Executive communication • Phased rollout strategies

**→ [Read the full guide](people/change-management.md)** | Templates: [Stakeholder Analysis](templates/shared/stakeholder-analysis.md)

---

### 10. Domain Expertise & Industry Specialization

**The Challenge:** Products often serve specific industries with unique terminology, regulations, and workflows. FDEs need enough domain knowledge to be credible while remaining adaptable.

**Core Topics:** Domain knowledge requirements • Ramp-up strategies • Leveraging domain experts • Industry-specific playbooks • Building domain credibility

**→ [Read the full guide](product/domain-expertise.md)**

---

### 11. Geographic Distribution & Remote Models

**The Challenge:** Talent may be concentrated in different locations than clients. Organizations must navigate trade-offs between talent access, cost, and client proximity.

**Core Topics:** Location strategy • Remote FDE effectiveness • Client site engagement • Distributed team coordination • Global client considerations

**→ [Read the full guide](people/geographic-distribution.md)**

---

### 12. Scaling the FDE Organization

**The Challenge:** What works at 5 FDEs often breaks at 30 or 50. Organizations need to design for scale while still moving fast in early stages.

**Core Topics:** Scaling milestones • Management layer introduction • Consistency at scale • Knowledge scaling • Process maturation • Infrastructure scaling

**→ [Read the full guide](operations/scaling-organization.md)**

---

### 13. Failure Recovery & Risk Management

**The Challenge:** Deployments will sometimes fail—products aren't ready, client expectations misaligned, technical issues arise. Organizations need playbooks for recovery and risk mitigation.

**Core Topics:** Common failure modes • Early warning systems • Recovery playbooks • Post-mortem practices • Risk mitigation strategies • Client relationship repair

**→ [Read the full guide](product/failure-recovery-risk.md)**

---

### 14. Legal, Contracts & IP Considerations

**The Challenge:** FDE work often involves building in client environments, using client data, and creating custom solutions. Clear legal frameworks protect both parties.

**Core Topics:** Intellectual property ownership • Liability & indemnification • Data handling & privacy • Contract structures • Confidentiality • Employment & subcontracting

**→ [Read the full guide](commercial/legal-contracts-ip.md)** | Templates: [MSA Template](templates/commercial/msa-template.md)

---

### 15. Partner & Ecosystem Coexistence

**The Challenge:** Clients often have existing relationships with system integrators, consultants, and internal teams. FDEs must collaborate effectively without stepping on toes.

**Core Topics:** System integrator relationships • Client internal teams • Technology partner ecosystems • Channel considerations • Competitive dynamics

**→ [Read the full guide](commercial/partner-ecosystem.md)**

---

### 16. FDE Community & Knowledge Management

**The Challenge:** FDEs embedded with different clients can become isolated. Systematic knowledge sharing multiplies the value of every lesson learned.

**Core Topics:** Community of practice • Knowledge capture • Internal communication • Mentorship programs • Continuous learning • Internal mobility

**→ [Read the full guide](people/community-knowledge-management.md)**

---

### 17. Competitive Positioning & Differentiation

**The Challenge:** Clients may not understand why FDE-led delivery differs from hiring a consulting firm or building internally. Clear positioning wins deals and sets appropriate expectations.

**Core Topics:** FDE value proposition • Differentiation from consulting firms • Differentiation from internal teams • Competitive battlecards • Client education • Thought leadership

**→ [Read the full guide](commercial/competitive-positioning.md)**

## Engagement Models

### Advisory Retainer
Ongoing strategic guidance through regular check-ins. Recommended cadence:
- **Setup Phase (Months 1-3):** 2x per week
- **Building Phase (Months 4-6):** Weekly
- **Scaling Phase (Months 7+):** Bi-weekly or as needed

### Workshop Facilitation
Intensive working sessions for specific practice areas. Typical formats:
- **Half-day:** Single topic deep-dive
- **Full-day:** Multiple related topics or action planning
- **Multi-day:** Comprehensive transformation design

### Embedded Advisory
Real-time coaching during active initiatives:
- Shadowing FDE implementations
- Interview panel participation
- Deal review and qualification
- Post-mortem facilitation

### Deliverable Development
Creation of custom materials for client context:
- Playbooks and process documentation
- Training curricula
- Templates and frameworks
- Assessment tools

### Assessment & Diagnostic
Structured evaluation of current state:
- FDE maturity assessment
- Gap analysis against best practices
- Prioritized improvement roadmap
- Quick win identification

## Key Success Factors

1. **Executive Sponsorship:** FDE transformation requires top-down support and willingness to reorganize

2. **Pain Tolerance:** Early FDEs must embrace ambiguity and undocumented systems—and hiring must select for this

3. **Customer Centricity:** All decisions prioritize customer outcomes over internal convenience

4. **Speed Over Perfection:** Ship working solutions, iterate based on feedback

5. **Documentation as Byproduct:** Learn by doing, document for the next person

6. **Technical Leadership Authority:** FDEs must have real ability to influence product/platform decisions

7. **Patience with Transition:** The shift from services to product-led takes 12-24 months to fully realize

8. **Willingness to Walk Away:** Not every deal is right for FDE engagement; discipline in qualification matters
