# FDE Advisory Templates

This directory contains ready-to-use templates, frameworks, and tools to support FDE transformation work. Templates are organized by theme to match the practice area structure.

## Template Organization

### `/people/` - Human Capital & Talent Templates
Templates for hiring, onboarding, training, career development, and organizational design.

### `/product/` - Product & Technical Templates
Templates for technical standards, product-FDE interfaces, and technical enablement.

### `/commercial/` - Revenue & Market Templates
Templates for commercial models, contracts, pricing, and customer success.

### `/operations/` - Operating Model Templates
Templates for metrics, governance, and scaling operations.

### `/shared/` - Cross-Cutting Templates
Templates that apply across multiple practice areas (RACI matrices, stakeholder analysis, etc.).

---

## How to Use These Templates

### 1. Find the Right Template
- Browse by theme folder based on your current focus area
- Each template includes a description of when and how to use it
- Templates are referenced from detailed practice area documents

### 2. Customize for Your Context
- All templates are starting points, not prescriptive solutions
- Adapt language, structure, and content to your organization
- Consider your company stage, industry, and culture

### 3. Iterate and Improve
- Templates should evolve based on what works in practice
- Document changes and improvements
- Share learnings back to the community

## Contributing Templates

Have a template that's working well for your FDE organization? We'd love to include it!

1. Anonymize any client-specific or sensitive information
2. Follow the naming convention: `{purpose}-{type}.md` or `.xlsx`
3. Include a clear description of when and how to use it
4. Add examples where helpful
5. Submit via pull request or issue

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for detailed contribution guidelines.

---

## Template Naming Conventions

**Format:** `{purpose}-{type}.{extension}`

**Examples:**
- `job-description-fde.md` - Job posting template for FDE roles
- `pilot-scoping-worksheet.md` - Pilot project scoping tool
- `metrics-dashboard-spec.md` - Dashboard specification
- `raci-matrix-template.md` - RACI responsibility matrix

**File Types:**
- `.md` - Markdown for documents and written templates
- `.xlsx` - Spreadsheets for calculators, matrices, scorecards (future)
- `.pptx` - Presentations for workshops and training (future)
