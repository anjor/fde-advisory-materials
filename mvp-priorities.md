# FDE Advisory Framework: MVP Priorities

## Executive Summary

This framework has 17 practice areas. For an MVP, we need **3-4 that solve immediate problems**. The rest should be archived until there's demand.

---

## Priority Selection Criteria

Based on Fractal engagement transcripts (Nov-Dec 2024), these are the burning problems:

| Problem | Evidence | Priority Area |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| Need to hire 3-5 FDEs by Feb | Dec 9: "What's the minimum people we need to hire... by Feb so that you and I don't lose our minds?" | **Hiring & Talent Strategy** |
| Product teams not delivering what FDEs need | Dec 11: "We are spending days just talking to six different people trying to get to an environment" | **Product-FDE Interface** |
| No documentation, scattered repos, can't work | Dec 11: "One repository is in GitHub, another is in Azure DevOps, another is on somebody's local machine" | **Technical Enablement** |
| Unclear when FDEs engage in sales cycle | Dec 4: "When does the FD start getting involved in the sales process?" | (Covered in Product-FDE Interface) |

---

## MVP Practice Areas (Priority Order)

### 1. Hiring & Talent Strategy
**Why Now:** Fractal is hiring immediately. Ajoy wants 3-5 FDEs by Feb 2025.
**What They Need:**
- Interview process (phone screen → tech → learning/reengineering → decomp → hiring manager)
- Evaluation criteria for FDE-specific traits
- Modified process for "circle of trust" referrals
- Fresh graduate handling

### 2. Product-FDE Interface  
**Why Now:** Single biggest source of friction. Product teams building demos, expecting FDEs to handle clients, but not providing FDEs with what they need to work.
**What They Need:**
- Clear ownership boundaries
- What FDEs can demand from Product teams
- When Product teams should act as FDEs (first 1-2 clients)
- Escalation process when Product isn't delivering

### 3. Technical Enablement
**Why Now:** FDEs literally cannot work. Environment setup takes days. No documentation exists.
**What They Need:**
- Minimum standards Product teams must meet
- Environment setup requirements
- Documentation checklist
- "Learn by doing, document immediately" workflow

## Deferred Practice Areas

### Phase 2 (After first 5 deployments)
- Operating Model & Metrics - Need data before optimizing
- Customer Deployment & Success - Need deployments first
- Training & Career Development - Need FDEs to train first

### Phase 3 (After 20+ FDEs)
- Scaling the Organization
- Community & Knowledge Management
- Geographic Distribution

### Archive (May never need)
- Competitive Positioning - Marketing concern, not operational
- Partner & Ecosystem - No current partner strategy
- Domain Expertise - Handle case-by-case
- Legal, Contracts & IP - Use existing legal team

## Success Criteria for MVP

The framework is "done enough" when:
- [ ] Fractal can run FDE interviews using provided materials
- [ ] FDEs know exactly what to demand from Product teams
- [ ] Product teams know exactly what they must deliver
- [ ] A new FDE can get a working environment in <1 day (not days)

Everything else is premature.
