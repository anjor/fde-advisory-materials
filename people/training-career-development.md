## The Challenge

Even great hires fail without proper onboarding, and retention suffers when career paths are unclear. Organizations need structured programs to develop and retain FDE talent.

**Key Considerations:**

- Weeks 2-4: Shadowing active FDE engagements
- Weeks 5-8: Supported first deployment with mentor oversight
- Weeks 9-12: Independent deployment with check-ins
- Milestone checkpoints and go/no-go decisions
