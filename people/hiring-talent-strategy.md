## Key Topics

### 3.1 Ideal Candidate Profile

**Key Considerations:**
- Technical depth combined with client-facing capabilities
- Ownership mindset and follow-through on commitments
- Ability to work across technical domains (full-stack generalists)
- Creative problem-solving under ambiguity
- High pain tolerance and grit
- Communication skills across technical and business audiences

### 3.3 Interview Process Design

**Key Considerations:**
- Phone screening for baseline fit
- Technical assessment (adapted based on candidate background)
- Learning & Reengineering exercises (ability to quickly master new domains)
- Decomposition exercises (breaking down complex problems)
- Hiring manager thesis development
- Behavioral assessment for pain tolerance and grit
